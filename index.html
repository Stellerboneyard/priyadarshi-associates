    FAQ

    GST Questions

    Any business with aggregate annual turnover exceeding ₹40 lakhs (₹20 lakhs for service providers) must register. E-commerce sellers and inter-state suppliers must register regardless of turnover.
    Late filing attracts a late fee of ₹50/day (₹20/day for nil returns), capped at ₹10,000. Additionally, interest at 18% p.a. is charged on unpaid tax. Our proactive filing ensures you never miss a due date.
    GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted statement showing ITC available based on suppliers' GSTR-1 filings. Matching your purchase register with GSTR-2B ensures you claim only eligible ITC and avoid demand notices.

    Eligibility

    Who Must File ITR?

    👔

    Salaried Employees

    If your gross salary exceeds ₹3 lakh (new regime) or ₹2.5 lakh (old regime). Also if you want to claim refund of TDS deducted by employer.

    🏢

    Business Owners

    All proprietors, partners, and directors must file ITR, regardless of profit or loss. Tax audit mandatory above ₹1 crore turnover.

    ✈️

    NRIs

    NRIs with Indian income (rent, dividends, capital gains, FD interest) must file ITR. Special rules for DTAA benefits apply.

    🏠

    Property Owners

    Anyone with rental income or who sold property during the year must report capital gains. LTCG exemptions available under Sec 54.

    📈

    Investors

    Mutual fund redemptions, stock sales, and FD maturity proceeds must be reported. STCG at 15%, LTCG at 10% above ₹1 lakh.

    🎓

    Students & Freelancers

    Any person with income above the threshold — including freelancers, consultants, and tuition teachers — must file ITR-3 or ITR-4.

    💡 Why file even if income is below the threshold?

    🏦 Loan Applications
    Banks require 2–3 years of ITR for home loans, business loans, and credit cards.
    🛂 Visa Applications
    US, UK, Schengen visas all ask for 3 years of ITR as financial proof of ties to India.
    💸 TDS Refund
    Banks and employers deduct TDS. Filing ITR is the only way to claim it back if not liable.
